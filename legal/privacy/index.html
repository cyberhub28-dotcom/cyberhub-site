CyberHub Website Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This website is operated by CyberHub Software, LLC, an Ohio limited liability company (“CyberHub,” “we,” “us”). This policy explains what we collect on cyberhubsoftware.com and through the CyberHub Template Shop, why, and what you can do about it.

What this site collects

This site has no analytics and no advertising cookies. Our host, GitHub Pages, keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security.

Free review form

Our free review form is a Google Form. If you fill it in, we collect what you enter there: your name, business name and contact details, what your business does, your current website if you have one, what you want from the new site, style and color preferences, budget, timeline and any message. Your answers go to Google Forms and are stored in our Google Workspace account, where Google handles them under its own privacy policy and terms. We use them only to reply to you and give you a quote, keep them as long as needed for that and our records, and never sell them or share them for advertising.

Email

If you email contact@ or support@cyberhubsoftware.com, your message goes to our Google Workspace mailbox. We use your name, email address and message only to reply and do the work you ask for, keep them as long as needed for that and our records, and never sell or share them for advertising.

Template Shop: Stripe checkout

Template Shop products (mini web apps only) are sold as one-time purchases through Stripe. When you buy, you enter your payment details on Stripe’s checkout, and Stripe handles your card and payment information under its own privacy policy. We do not see or store your full card number.

From Stripe, we receive the order details we need to fulfill your purchase: your email address, the amount paid, the product id, the Stripe payment id, and payment status (for example, paid, refunded, or disputed).

Template Shop: one shared purchase-key server (Cloudflare)

All Template Shop mini apps use one shared purchase-key server: a single Cloudflare Worker that unlocks every mini app. After payment, it creates your personal purchase key and link, and your key record says which app you bought. To deliver and enforce your license, it stores only:

  • A scrambled copy of your purchase key (we do not keep the key itself in readable form)
  • Your buyer email
  • Which app you bought (app id)
  • The purchase date
  • Your device count (how many devices you have activated, maximum 3)
  • Scrambled device tokens (a random token is saved in your browser when you activate a device; our server keeps only a scrambled copy, to enforce the 3-device limit)
  • Your Stripe payment id (links your key to your order for refunds and chargebacks)
  • Your key status (for example, active or disabled)

That is the complete list. The purchase-key server stores no card data (Stripe handles that) and none of what you enter in a mini app. Your unlock email (your key and personal link) is sent to your buyer email through our email provider, Resend. Cloudflare may also process standard request information, such as IP address and browser type, when your device contacts the shared purchase-key server, for security and to deliver the service.

No account and no server for your content. Template Shop mini apps (including Budget and Credentials Tracker) have no account. CyberHub never stores buyer content. Everything you enter in a mini app, including photos and documents, stays only on your device (in your browser’s storage) and is never sent to CyberHub. CyberHub cannot see, recover, restore, or delete that data; it is under your control. Clearing browser or site data, deleting the Home Screen app, or switching phones without a backup can erase it (see our Template License Agreement).

The shared purchase-key server described above stores license records only (the items listed above). It never receives or stores what you enter in a mini app, so “no server” for your content does not conflict with the shared purchase-key server. The mini apps contact only that purchase-key server (to check your key and device), plus, for apps with PDF tools, cdnjs as a backup source for the PDF tool’s code; neither receives your app content.

Backup and reminders. Each mini app includes a Backup button that saves one file with all your data, including photos and documents. You create that file on your device and choose where to keep it (for example, Files, iCloud Drive, or Google Drive); it is how you restore your data later, and it is not sent to CyberHub. If you keep it with a cloud service, that service’s own terms and privacy policy apply. The backup file is not encrypted: anyone who has it can open it, so keep it private. The app shows a backup reminder when you open it if you haven't backed up in a week; the reminder runs on your device and sends nothing to CyberHub.

Template Shop: why we use this information

We use Template Shop information only to:

  • Fulfill your purchase and deliver your key and link
  • Enforce your license, including the 3-device limit
  • Prevent casual sharing of keys and links
  • Handle refunds, chargebacks, and support requests
  • Keep tax, accounting, and other legally required records

Template Shop: how long we keep it

We keep purchase and key records as long as needed to provide your access, meet tax and accounting requirements, handle refunds and chargebacks, and comply with law. After that, we delete or minimize them (for example, removing device identifiers we no longer need).

You can ask us to see or delete your Template Shop information at any time. See “Your choices: access and deletion requests” below for how to ask, how fast we respond, and what deletion means for your purchase key.

Service providers

We use these service providers to run the site and the Template Shop. Each handles information under its own terms and privacy policy:

  • Stripe — payment processing and checkout
  • Cloudflare — the one shared purchase-key server (a single Worker for all mini apps) and related security
  • Resend — sends the unlock email with your purchase key and personal link (it receives your email address and that email’s content)
  • cdnjs — a public third-party script host that some mini apps may load PDF tooling code from as a backup. The app only downloads code from it and never sends your content to it; like any website, it may see standard request information such as IP address
  • Google Workspace — email, Google Forms, and business records
  • GitHub Pages — website hosting and server logs

No sale of personal information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

Do Not Track

We don't respond to Do Not Track signals because we don't track you across sites.

Children

This site and the Template Shop aren't directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your choices: access and deletion requests

How to ask. To see, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, email contact@cyberhubsoftware.com or support@cyberhubsoftware.com, or write to CyberHub Software, LLC, P.O. Box 160, Alpha, OH 45301. Tell us what you want and the email address you used with us (for Template Shop requests, the email you used at checkout). We may ask you to confirm the request from that email address so we know it is really you.

What it covers. Requests cover our website form and email records and our Template Shop purchase and purchase-key server records (the fields listed above). We never receive what you enter in a mini app, so we cannot see or delete it; you control it on your device and can delete it there.

How fast. We aim to respond within 2 business days, and to complete access and deletion requests within 45 days where the law requires a deadline.

What deletion means. Your purchase key depends on its key-server record, so deleting your Template Shop records may disable your purchase key and end your access to the mini app. We may keep limited order and payment records (for example, tax, accounting, refund, and chargeback records) where the law requires it, and we will tell you if we do.
