This Template Shop Refund Policy (“Policy”) applies to digital products sold in the CyberHub Template Shop on cyberhubsoftware.com, which are mini web apps only (PWA-style apps you can add to your phone’s Home Screen, unlocked by a personal purchase key) and bundles of those apps (each a “Template Product”).

Template Products are sold through Stripe as a one-time purchase. There are no subscriptions. By purchasing a Template Product, you agree to this Policy and to our Template License Agreement.

1. How Template Products are delivered

After your Stripe payment succeeds, we create a personal purchase key and personal link for you. The first time you open the link, the mini-app checks your key and remembers that device. Each key works on up to 3 devices. Your key and link are the complete delivery: the key unlocks the mini web app you bought.

Because access is delivered instantly and digital products can be copied, sales are generally final once your key and link have been delivered to you, except as described in Section 2.

Please read the product page carefully before you buy. Prices are in U.S. dollars unless otherwise stated.

2. When we will refund (or fix it)

We will provide a refund, or at our option a fix, re-delivery, or replacement, if:

  • Failed delivery. You paid successfully but did not receive a working personal link or purchase key within a reasonable time (usually a few hours), or your key never activated on your first device because of a problem on our side, and you contact us promptly.
  • Wrong product. We delivered a different Template Product than the one you ordered.
  • Corrupt or unusable. The mini-app will not load or work as described on the product page on a supported, up-to-date browser, through no fault of your device setup, and you tell us within 7 days of purchase with enough detail for us to reproduce the problem.
  • Duplicate charge. You were accidentally charged twice for the same order. We refund the duplicate charge.
  • Required by law. A refund is required under consumer-protection law that applies to you.

Before refunding a delivery or technical problem, we may first try to fix it (for example, by issuing a new purchase key and personal link from our key server). We do not re-send an old link: a replacement key means the old key stops working.

3. When we will not refund

We generally do not refund when:

  • Change of mind after activation. You changed your mind after your key was activated.
  • Wrong choice by you. You bought the wrong product or bundle (unless we delivered the wrong item).
  • It matched the description. You are unhappy with design, layout, or features that matched the product page.
  • Your device or platform. The product does not work because of your device, operating system, browser, storage settings, or third-party changes outside our control.
  • Lost on-device data. You lost data you entered in a mini app, for example by clearing browser or site data, deleting or uninstalling the Home Screen app, or switching phones without a backup (see Section 5).
  • License breach. You violated the Template License Agreement (for example, by sharing your key or link, or trying to exceed the 3-device limit).

4. What happens when we refund: your key is disabled

When we issue a refund for any reason, we disable your purchase key and revoke your access to that Template Product promptly, normally the same day the refund is issued. Your personal link will stop working on all devices, and your license to that product ends. A chargeback decided in your favor disables your key the same way (see Section 7).

Before you accept a refund, use the mini-app’s Backup button and save the backup file if you want to keep your data.

5. Your data stays on your device

Information you enter in a mini app (including Budget and Credentials Tracker), including photos and documents, is stored only on your device. There is no account. CyberHub does not store it and cannot see, recover, or restore it.

Your data can be erased by actions on your device, for example:

  • iPhone: deleting the Home Screen app, or switching phones without a backup.
  • iPhone, regular Safari tab: clearing website data, or Safari clearing a tab’s data after about 7 days without use (the Home Screen version is not affected by clearing Safari history or that 7-day rule).
  • Android: clearing Chrome’s cookies and site data (included by default in Clear browsing data), which erases your data even from the Home Screen app; uninstalling the app; or switching phones without a backup.
  • Any device: losing or resetting your device without a backup.

Data typed in a browser tab does not move to the Home Screen version by itself; use Backup and Restore.

Each mini app includes a Backup button that saves one file with all your data, including photos and documents; that file is how you restore your data later. The app shows a backup reminder when you open it if you haven't backed up in a week. Making backups and keeping that file somewhere safe (for example, Files, iCloud Drive, or Google Drive) is your responsibility. The backup file is not encrypted, so anyone who has it can open it; keep it private.

Loss of on-device data in any of these ways is not, by itself, grounds for a refund after your key has been successfully activated. These risks are disclosed before purchase and in our Template License Agreement.

We aim to respond within 2 business days. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method through Stripe; your bank’s timing may vary.

7. Chargebacks

Please contact us first so we can fix delivery or access problems quickly. If you file a chargeback or payment dispute, we may suspend your purchase key while the dispute is open, and if the chargeback is decided in your favor, we will disable the key permanently, the same as a refund. Chargebacks we believe are unwarranted may also affect future purchases.

8. Bundles and promotions

Refunds for bundles are handled for the bundle as a whole. A partial refund on a bundle (for example, when only one app in it had a delivery, wrong-product, or corrupt/unusable problem) is never automatic: a person at CyberHub reviews each partial-refund request and decides it case by case. If a partial refund is approved, we disable only the key or keys for the refunded app or apps. If the whole bundle is refunded, every key in the bundle is disabled. Discounted or promotional purchases follow the same rules unless the offer says otherwise.
