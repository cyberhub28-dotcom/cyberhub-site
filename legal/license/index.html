  • No account, no server for your content. CyberHub mini apps (including Budget and Credentials Tracker) have no account and no server for your content. Everything you enter, including photos and documents, stays only on your device (in your browser’s storage). It is not sent to or stored by CyberHub, and CyberHub cannot see, recover, or restore it. Our shared purchase-key server is separate: it stores only the license records needed to deliver and enforce your license (a scrambled copy of your purchase key, your buyer email, which app you bought (app id), purchase date, device count (maximum 3), scrambled device tokens, Stripe payment id, and key status). It stores no card data and never your app content (see our Privacy Policy).
  • Supported devices. The mini apps work on iPhone (Safari) and Android (Chrome) and are best used from the Home Screen.
  • iPhone. Deleting the Home Screen app, or switching phones without a backup, erases your data. In a regular Safari tab, clearing website data erases your data, and Safari may clear a tab’s data after about 7 days without use. The Home Screen version is not affected by clearing Safari history or that 7-day rule.
  • Android. The Home Screen app shares storage with Chrome, so clearing Chrome’s cookies and site data (included by default in Clear browsing data) erases your data, even from the Home Screen. Uninstalling the app, or switching phones without a backup, also erases it.
  • Browser tab vs. Home Screen. Data typed in a browser tab does not move to the Home Screen version by itself. Use Backup and Restore to move it.
  • Backup button. Each mini app includes a Backup button that saves one file with all your data, including photos and documents. That file is how you restore your data later. You are responsible for making backups and keeping that file somewhere safe (for example, Files, iCloud Drive, or Google Drive). The app shows a backup reminder when you open it if you haven't backed up in a week.
  • Backup file is not encrypted. Anyone who has your backup file can open it, so keep it private.
  • Storage. Large photos and PDFs use phone storage and make the backup file bigger.
  • We cannot recover lost data. CyberHub never stores your content and cannot see, recover, or restore it for you. If your data is erased in any of the ways above, you lose or reset your device, or your key is disabled, use your Backup file to restore. Without that file, your data may be lost permanently (see Sections 8 and 9).
