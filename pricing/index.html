FAQ

Straight answers.

Anything else? Email contact@cyberhubsoftware.com.

Is it really one price, with nothing monthly?

Yes. You pay the flat price once (50% deposit to start, 50% at launch, before the site goes live). We don't sell monthly plans. Your only ongoing cost is your own domain renewal. Your site is hosted free on GitHub Pages.

How do you get access to my accounts?

We never ask for your passwords. You invite us instead: as a Manager on your Google Business Profile, or as a contributor on your current website platform. For new builds, we work on our side and send you a preview link. After final payment, we move the site into your own account or walk you through pointing your domain. At handoff, you remove our access, and we’ll show you how.

Who owns the website?

You do. Your domain and hosting are in your name from day one. When you pay the final invoice, ownership of the custom design and code transfers to you under our short written agreement. Stock photos and fonts stay under their own free licenses, which cover your use. At hand-off you hold every login and we remove our access.

What is the 90-day fix window?

Every package includes it. For 90 days after launch, we fix anything that isn't working or isn't what we agreed, free. After that, small updates are $50 each and bigger work is a new quote, with no retainer.

Can I edit the site myself?

Yes. We set up a simple editor so you can change text, photos and events yourself, and we show you how. Design and layout changes come back to us.

How long does it take?

About one week for Starter and one to three weeks for Standard and Plus. The biggest factor is how quickly we get your photos, hours and service details.

What do you need from me?

Your current web address, your logo if you have one, any photos you'd like used, and a few minutes to confirm hours, services and prices. We can use free-license stock photos where you don't have your own.

Do you only work in Dayton?

We're based in the Dayton area and focus on the Miami Valley and small-town Ohio, but we can work with businesses anywhere by email and video call.
