Payment & Refund Terms

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This website is operated by CyberHub Software, LLC, an Ohio limited liability company (“CyberHub,” “we,” “us”).

Payments. Projects are billed 50% to start and 50% when you approve the finished site. Invoices are due on receipt and can be paid by card or bank transfer.

Deposit. Your deposit reserves your project and pays for the design work. If you cancel before we start work, we refund it in full. Once work has begun, the deposit is non-refundable because it covers work already done.

Final payment. Due when you approve the finished site. Ownership of the custom design and code passes to you when it's paid.

Revisions and cancellation. The revisions included in your project are listed in your written agreement. If you cancel after work begins, you owe nothing more, and the unfinished design stays ours unless you pay the final invoice.

Questions or problems. Email support@cyberhubsoftware.com and we'll respond within 2 business days. Please contact us before disputing a charge with your bank.

These terms work alongside your written project agreement. If the two conflict, your signed agreement controls.
