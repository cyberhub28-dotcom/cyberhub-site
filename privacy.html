Privacy (cyberhubsoftware.com)

Effective October 18, 2026

What this site collects

This site has no analytics and no advertising cookies. Our host, GitHub Pages, keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security.

Free review form

If you fill in our free review form, we collect what you enter: your name, business name and email, and, only if you choose to add them, your phone number, current website address (or that you don't have one), business type, what you want most, budget range, timeline, preferred contact method and any message. The form sends these through our form service, Web3Forms, which emails them to contact@cyberhubsoftware.com, and Cloudflare Email Routing forwards them to our mailbox at Google Gmail. Web3Forms and Cloudflare handle this data only to deliver it, under their own privacy policies, and receive your IP address when you submit. Web3Forms keeps a copy of each submission for 3 months, then deletes it. We use them only to reply to you and give you a quote, keep them as long as needed for that and our records, and never sell them or share them for advertising.

Email

If you email contact@ or support@cyberhubsoftware.com, Cloudflare Email Routing forwards your message to our mailbox at Google Gmail. We use your name, email address and message only to reply and do the work you ask for, keep them as long as needed for that and our records, and never sell or share them for advertising.

Do Not Track

We don't respond to Do Not Track signals because we don't track you across sites.

Children

This site isn't directed to children under 13.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete what we hold, email contact@cyberhubsoftware.com.
