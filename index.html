FAQ

Straight answers.

Anything else? Email contact@cyberhubsoftware.com.

Is it really one price, with nothing monthly?

Yes. You pay the flat price once (half to start, half at approval). We don't sell monthly plans. Your only ongoing costs are your own domain renewal and, if you choose Squarespace or Wix, that platform's plan. GitHub Pages and Cloudflare Pages hosting are free.

Who owns the website?

You do. Your domain and hosting are in your name from day one. When you pay the final invoice, ownership of the custom design and code transfers to you under our short written agreement. Stock photos and fonts stay under their own free licenses, which cover your use. At hand-off you hold every login and we remove our access.

What is the 30-day fix window?

For 30 days after launch, we fix anything that isn't working or isn't what we agreed, free. After that, new work is a new quote, with no retainer.

Can I edit the site myself?

Yes. If you want to change things often, we can build it on Squarespace or Wix in your account and show you how. If you rarely change anything, a fast static site on free hosting is usually the better fit.

How long does it take?

About one week for Starter and one to three weeks for Standard and Plus. The biggest factor is how quickly we get your photos, hours and service details.

What do you need from me?

Your current web address, your logo if you have one, any photos you'd like used, and a few minutes to confirm hours, services and prices. We can use free-license stock photos where you don't have your own.

Do you only work in Dayton?

We're based in the Dayton area and focus on the Miami Valley and small-town Ohio, but we can work with businesses anywhere by email and video call.
